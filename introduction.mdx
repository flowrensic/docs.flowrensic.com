Every workflow leaves a trace. Flowrensic keeps what your Temporal server recorded and follows a workflow across its whole lifecycle: the code, the deploys it goes through, and the runs it produces across environments.

<Note>
  Flowrensic is pre-launch.
</Note>

## What it does

  <Card title="Replay" icon="rotate" href="/concepts/replay">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Node SDK" icon="node-js" href="/sdk/node">

  </Card>
  <Card title="API" icon="code" href="/api-reference/overview">

  </Card>
</CardGroup>
