---
title: "Catalog"
description: "Deploy environments, servers and namespaces."
---

The catalog is three levels:

- A **deploy environment** holds servers.
- A **server** holds namespaces.
- A **namespace** is what a worker is pointed at.

Metrics belong to the server, so one scrape serves every namespace on it. Temporal is the only engine supported, so nothing in the catalog names one.
